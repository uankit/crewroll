This update improves trip approval, photo-access recovery, and navigation. Join requests now appear in the crew list before a trip starts, with late requests in notifications.

Please use CrewRoll 1.0.4 on both phones. This release uses production accounts; older beta accounts and trips are separate.

What to try:
- Host a trip on iPhone and join from Android, then try the reverse.
- Approve the invited member in the crew list. A guest with photo access already allowed should enter the trip and become ready without reopening the app.
- Take a new photo with your phone's usual camera. Confirm the original saves in the other phone's photo library.
- Invite someone after the trip starts. Approve them in notifications and confirm they receive eligible photos taken after they join.
- Reopen after going offline or closing the app and check that sharing resumes.
- Go back from the invite preview and photo explanation, then return to the trip.
- Open Account to review privacy options. In a trip, open a photo or member to find reporting and blocking.

Keep both apps open while checking delivery. Background work depends on iOS and Android scheduling; force-closing an app can delay sharing. Keep your own photo backups.

Send feedback through TestFlight or support@crewroll.app. Include your phone model, app version and what happened. Do not send private photos, passwords or verification codes.
