# Google Play listing

## App name

CrewRoll: Shared Trip Photos

## Short description

Your crew’s trip photos, shared privately and saved to your phone.

## Full description

CrewRoll brings your trip photos together in one private group.

BRING YOUR CREW
Create a trip, share its invite code, and approve the friends who join. CrewRoll works between iPhone and Android. Each member needs the app and an account.

KEEP USING YOUR CAMERA
Take photos with your phone’s usual camera. With full photo access enabled, CrewRoll finds eligible new photos from the active trip and shares them with approved members. Your whole photo library is not uploaded.

KEEP THE ORIGINALS
Photos are encrypted on your phone before transfer. CrewRoll checks each original before saving it to your phone’s photo library. The trip gallery shows what this phone is uploading, saving, or waiting to sync.

SHARE AT YOUR PACE
Pause your own sharing, choose whether to use mobile data, and return to a trip after closing the app. Friends approved after the trip starts receive eligible photos taken after they join.

PRIVATE TO YOUR CREW
There is no public feed or advertising. You can report a photo or member, block someone, and delete your account from the app or at crewroll.app/delete-account. Photos already saved to someone’s library remain there.

Open CrewRoll to help transfers finish and check that photos have saved. Background activity depends on your phone’s operating system, permissions, storage, and connection. Temporary encrypted copies expire; keep your own photo backups.
